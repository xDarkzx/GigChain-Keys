# Loop station

Record a few bars of one instrument, let it loop, and play over it: street
performer style. Every channel can have its own loop, and you can stack layers
on top.

## Show the looper

**Loops > Show the looper strip** (top bar) shows a row of loop buttons above
the mixer's channel strips: one per channel.

## Record a loop

1. Click the loop button of the channel you want to record (**Record a loop of
   this channel**).
2. Play.
3. Click again to **close the loop**. It starts playing round at once.

Click once more to **record a layer on top**, and again to end the layer.
Right-click the loop button to **Undo last layer** or **Clear this loop**.

## Loops in time with the song

**Loops > Loops follow the tempo (bars)** makes every loop a whole number of
bars, in time with the song's tempo. Choose the length under **Loop length**:
*1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars* (the loop closes by itself at the end), or **Open**
(it closes where you stop it). Recording starts on the next bar.

**Stopped early?** With the length set to 4 bars, stop after 1 bar and your
bar plays four times to fill the 4 bars. The loop is still 4 bars long, so a
layer you record on top can be a 4-bar line over your 1-bar riff. (Stopped
in bar 3 of 4, it keeps 2 bars, played twice.)

**Notes that ring past the end** are not cut off. Held chords, a press a
little after the bar line and reverb tails carry on smoothly over the start of
the loop each time it comes round. The loop's start is faded in very slightly,
so it never clicks.

Without tempo sync, **Free loops: the first sets the tempo** makes the first
loop you record set the tempo for the others.

## Stop and clear

- **Loops > Stop all loops** stops them all.
- **Loops > Clear all loops** throws them all away.
- When the looper strip is hidden, a small **loops** display in the top bar
  shows what they are doing.

## From your keyboard

**Loops > Keyboard controls…** lets you learn a button, pad or pedal for
**Record** and **Loop**, so your hands never leave the keys. They act on the
instrument lit in the mixer; holding Record and Loop together clears its loop.
A knob can **choose the instrument**. These are kept with the setlist.
