# Perform mode

**Perform** is the stage view. Click **Perform** in the top bar (or press
**Tab**). The window goes full screen and shows only what you need while you
play, big enough to read from the keys. Nothing in it can change your setlist
by accident.

## What you see

- **The header:** ◀ the previous song, the **song's name** and its sound, what
  comes **Next**, and ▶ the next song.
- **The song's parts** as tiles (Intro, Verse, Chorus…). The one playing is
  lit; tap a tile to jump there.
- **The chart**, filling the screen. While the song plays it scrolls by itself
  so the line you are on stays in the upper third.
- **The master level** and how the computer is doing (**CPU**), small at the
  bottom.

## Make the chart bigger or smaller

**A−** and **A+** change the chart's text size. Your choice is remembered for
next time.

## Moving through the night

| How | Does |
|---|---|
| ◀ ▶ in the header | Previous / next song |
| **Page Up** / **Page Down** | Previous / next song |
| **Space** or **→** / **←** | Next / previous sound |
| A tile | That part of the song |
| A pedal or pad | Whatever you learned it to do (Settings > MIDI > **Pedals and pads**) |

## Panic

The red **Panic** button (top bar) stops every stuck note at once. Use it if a
note keeps sounding after you let go.

## The mixer and keyboard on stage

In Perform mode the mixer and the on-screen keyboard are hidden so the chart
gets the room. The **Mixer** and **Keys** buttons bring them back; Perform and
Edit each remember their own choice.

## A song with no chart

The view says **No lyrics or chords for this song**. Click **Add lyrics &
chords** to go to the chart editor. See [Chord charts](charts.md).

## Back to Edit

Click **Edit**, or press **Esc** or **Tab**.
