# Code of conduct

GigChain Keys is made for musicians, by musicians. Everyone is welcome here,
whatever their experience, background, identity or the music they play.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are players, not programmers.
  There are no stupid questions.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- **Be helpful.** A clear bug report, a tested fix or a friendly answer to
  someone's question all make the project better.
- **Assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or hateful or discriminatory language or images.
- Personal attacks or threats.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Spam, advertising, or pirated software and plugins (cracked plugins are
  not supported, and links to them are removed).

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainer privately through GitHub:
use the **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or open a private
security report (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) if it is serious. Reports are
kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who
break these rules, for as long as needed.
