Carla vs GigChain Keys: which free plugin host for live keys?

Both are free, open source and run on Windows, Mac and Linux, so they often come up together. They're built for different jobs: Carla is a toolbox for hosting and routing plugins; GigChain Keys is a live rig for keyboard players, organised by songs.

Choose Carla if…

You want to host LV2 or older Linux plugin formats, wire audio and MIDI through a patchbay, bridge Windows plugins on Linux, or load a rack of plugins inside your DAW. It's a superb, flexible tool for building your own setup.

Choose GigChain Keys if…

You play keys in a band or solo and want what MainStage gives Mac players: a setlist where every song has its sounds, layers and splits set up from a menu, one press to change sound with no gap, the song's chord chart on screen, and pianos, synths and pads ready before you own a single plugin.

GigChain Keys is free: no ads, no account, no licence key.
