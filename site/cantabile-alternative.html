A free Cantabile alternative, with setlists, on any computer

Cantabile is a respected Windows live host, and its Lite edition is free. Two things send players looking elsewhere: set lists and keyboard splits come with the paid Solo edition (song states with Performer), and it doesn't run on Mac or Linux. GigChain Keys gives you setlists and splits free, on any computer.

What you get in GigChain Keys

  • A setlist of songs, each with its own sounds, loaded up front so changing song is instant and reverb tails ring on.
  • Channels like a mixer: layers, keyboard splits, transpose and effects.
  • Chord charts: paste a song from any chord site and each chord lands on its word; Perform mode shows it big.
  • Your plugins: VST3 and VST2 instruments and effects, from Kontakt to Surge XT and Vital.
  • Built-in sounds for a light rig: pianos, Rhodes, supersaw synths, pads and a SoundFont player.

When Cantabile is the better pick

If you're on Windows, already know Cantabile and rely on its routing, bindings and long track record, its paid editions are a solid buy. If you want setlists free, or you play on a Mac or Linux, try GigChain Keys.

GigChain Keys is free: no ads, no account, no licence key.
