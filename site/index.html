Everything a keys player needs on stage

Load your sounds once, and the whole night is ready.

Plays your plugins

Your VST3 and VST2 instruments and effects. Every sound in the setlist is loaded up front, so changing songs is instant and held notes ring on.

Setlists

All the night's songs in one file, in order, each with its own sounds. Change songs from the keyboard, a pedal or a pad.

Chord charts

Paste a song from any chord website and each chord lands on its word. Big enough to read from the keys.

Splits and layers

Key zones, transpose and velocity layers. Each part of the song (verse, chorus, solo) can switch which sounds play.

Perform mode

Full screen for the stage and touch screens: the chart, big Play, Next part and Loop part buttons that land on the bar line.

Its own instruments

Saw Stack Pro (EDM supersaws) and Piano Pro (a real recorded grand and upright), with sidechain pumping built in. No plugin to buy.

Practice and warm-ups

The song's chords fall onto a keyboard, YouTube-piano style. Daily warm-ups at your level, every run scored.

Loop station

Record a loop of any instrument in time with the song and layer on top, street-performer style.

Built not to fail

Plugins are scanned in a separate process, a crashing plugin is switched off, and an unplugged keyboard comes back by itself.

"Windows protected your PC"?

macOS

macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)

Disk imageDrag GigChain Keys to Applications
macOS won't open it?

The alpha isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Linux

64-bit: Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora, Arch, Mint…

AppImageOne file for most distributions .debUbuntu, Debian, Mint, Pop!_OS
How to run the AppImage

Make it executable, then run it: chmod +x GigChainKeys-*.AppImage. If it says FUSE is missing, install libfuse2.

You'll also want a MIDI keyboard. Two instruments come built in, and free ones cover pianos, synths and pads. Every release lists checksums and notes on the releases page.
