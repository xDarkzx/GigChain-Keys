A free Gig Performer alternative for keyboard players

Gig Performer is a well-loved, paid live plugin host for Windows and Mac. If you want the same idea (your plugins, a setlist, one press to change sound) without paying, or you play on Linux, GigChain Keys is a free option made for keyboard players.

Where Gig Performer shines

Gig Performer's rackspaces let you wire plugins together any way you like, and its scripting language can automate almost anything. Players with big, unusual rigs (several keyboards, guitar rigs, complex MIDI routing) and those who like building their own setup in detail get a lot from it.

Where GigChain Keys fits

GigChain Keys works like MainStage: each song has its sounds on channels, with a mixer, layers, splits and effects, and Perform mode moves to the next sound with one press and no gap. There's nothing to wire up. It also carries each song's chord and lyric chart, has pianos, synths and pads built in for when you don't own plugins yet, and a practice mode with daily warm-ups. It's free, with no ads, no account and no licence key, and it runs on Linux too.

How to choose

  • You want songs-and-sounds, set up in minutes, free, on any computer: GigChain Keys.
  • You want to wire every plugin and script your rig, and don't mind paying: Gig Performer.
  • You're not sure: try GigChain Keys first; it costs nothing. Your VST3 and VST2 plugins work in both (Audio Units on a Mac only in Gig Performer).
