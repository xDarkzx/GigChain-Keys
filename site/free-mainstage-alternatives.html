Free MainStage alternatives for Windows, Mac & Linux

MainStage is Apple's live rig for keyboard players, and it only runs on a Mac. If you play on Windows or Linux, or just don't want to pay, here is every free option, what each one is good at, and where each one stops.

GigChain Keys

A live rig made for keyboard players, built to work like MainStage on Windows, Mac and Linux. You build a setlist of songs, give each song its sounds (your VST3 and VST2 plugins, or the built-in pianos, synths, pads and SoundFont player), layer and split them across the keyboard, and in Perform mode one press moves to the next sound with no gap. Each song can carry its chord and lyric chart, shown big on stage. There's also a practice mode with daily warm-ups.

Good for: cover bands, function and wedding gigs, solo keys players, anyone who wants MainStage's songs-and-sounds way of working without a Mac. Not for: recording and editing tracks; it's a live tool, not a DAW.

Cantabile Lite

A long-running, well-respected Windows live host. The Lite edition is free and handles plugins, layers and splits well. Setlists, song states and the rest of the live-show tools come with the paid Solo and Performer editions, and it doesn't run on Mac or Linux.

Element

An open-source plugin host for Windows, Mac and Linux. You connect plugins together in a graph, which makes it flexible for building effects chains and custom routing. It has no setlist of songs or charts; switching sounds between songs is up to you.

Carla

A free, open-source plugin host, strongest on Linux, with patchbay routing and support for many plugin formats. Like Element, it's a toolbox for hosting and routing rather than a set of songs to play through on stage.

How to choose

  • You want MainStage's way of working (songs, sounds, one press to change) on Windows or Linux, free: GigChain Keys.
  • You're on Windows and happy to pay later for a mature, Windows-only tool: try Cantabile Lite, then its paid editions.
  • You want to wire up plugin chains yourself: Element or Carla.

GigChain Keys is free: no ads, no account, no licence key.

Questions

What is the best free alternative to MainStage?

For keyboard players who want setlists, layers and splits, chord charts and instant sound changes for free, on any computer, GigChain Keys is the closest match. Cantabile Lite is free on Windows but keeps setlists for its paid editions.

Is there a free MainStage for Windows?

MainStage itself is Mac only. On Windows you can use GigChain Keys, Cantabile Lite, Element or Carla, all free. See MainStage for Windows: what to use instead.

Can I use my VST plugins live without a DAW?

Yes, and it's usually better on stage: lower latency, fewer things to go wrong. See how to play VST plugins live without a DAW.
